FSOT 3-node Chua ring

Afternoon breadboard. Three identical analog oscillators in a triangle. Coupling pots at 11.12 kΩ must lock. At 20 kΩ they must not. Pin D1D38A. Zero free parameters.

1. Why this is the experiment

FSOT trits are (−1, 0, +1). A Chua node’s voltage already occupies three regions around ±1 V. The ESP32 12-bit ADC is the observer. The prediction is not a fitted onset: it is the golden-ratio operating point of three identical nodes on a closed loop.

σ = φ   →   Rc = α R / φ = 10 × 1800 / φ = 11124.61 Ω

Set all three pots there with a DMM. That is the lock test.

4. ESP32 placement (DOIT DevKit V1, USB up)

5. Triangle and bias

Bias per node (the only ESP32 interface):

VC1 -- 100k --+-- GPIO 34/35/32
              +-- 100k to GND
              +-- 100k to 3V3
              + 1N4148 to 3V3 and to GND

vADC = 1.65 + 0.5 × VC1. A ±2 V swing stays in 0.65–2.65 V.

6. Build order

  1. Seat three TL082s. Wire ±9 V rails. Do not connect batteries yet.
  2. Build three identical tanks (R, L, C1, C2, NIC). They must be copies.
  3. Triangle the three pots on the three VC1 nets.
  4. Bias networks. USB-power the ESP32. DMM each ADC node ≈ 1.65 V.
  5. Flash firmware. Confirm FSOT_RLC_HARDWARE_BOOT=ok.
  6. Then connect 9 V. If the DevKit resets, a clamp is missing — disconnect.
  7. DMM pots to 11.12 kΩ. Watch UART.

7. The two tests

TestPotsMust see
Unlocked20 kΩ (σ ≈ 0.9 < φ)FSOT_RLC_LOCK=0, trits mix
Locked11.12 kΩ (σ = φ)FSOT_RLC_LOCK=1, three trits equal

You do not tune a pot until a residual vanishes. The prediction is the 11.12 kΩ setting.
